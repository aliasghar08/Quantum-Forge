Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines the policies and procedures of Quantum Forge ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), founded and maintained by Ali Asghar (aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com), regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and technical data when you use our web application at quantom-forge.web.app.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. When you interact with Quantum Forge:

  • Account Data: If you choose to sign in using Firebase Authentication or Passkey/WebAuthn, we securely process your email address and authentication credentials. Quantum Forge does not store plain-text passwords.
  • Simulation & Research Data: Chemical structures, XYZ/CJSON coordinates, and reaction simulations are computed locally in your browser memory or synced to your private Cloud Firestore account if you choose to save them.
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's localStorage to persist user interface settings (e.g., dark theme selection, active force-field options, animation speed) locally on your device.

2. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdSense & Google Ads)

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-9402443776639153) and Google Ads Conversion Tracking (Tag: AW-16732994716) to support the ongoing development and hosting of this computational platform.

  • Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.

3. Third-Party Services

Quantum Forge utilizes trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure web hosting, Cloud Firestore databases, and Firebase Authentication. Firebase complies with rigorous privacy standards, SOC certifications, and ISO/IEC 27001 compliance.
  • NGL & WebGL: Molecular rendering runs entirely client-side on your local hardware acceleration graphics card. No molecular coordinate data is transmitted to external unauthorized third parties.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks, you have the right to:

  • Access any personal data associated with your account.
  • Request immediate deletion of your account and saved simulation templates.
  • Clear your local browser cache and localStorage at any time through your browser settings.

To request complete account or data deletion, contact us directly at aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com.
