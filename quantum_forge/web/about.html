About Quantum Forge

A unified web workstation for quantum chemistry, transition state optimization, machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIP), and comprehensive pharmacopeia curricula.

Platform Overview

Quantum Forge is an advanced, zero-install computational chemistry research dashboard built with Flutter Web, WebGL, and state-of-the-art machine learning surrogates. Designed and developed by Ali Asghar, the platform provides seamless transition state finding, dynamic nudged elastic band (NEB) reaction trajectory visualization, force-field relaxation, and comprehensive multi-format chemical structure I/O with bidirectional parity to desktop Avogadro 2.

Unlike traditional desktop-bound quantum chemistry software, Quantum Forge runs directly in the browser across Windows, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS, accelerating research and education worldwide.

Integrated Curricula & Clinical Pharmacopeia

Quantum Forge serves as an educational benchmark compendium and computational laboratory for students and professionals across three rigorous disciplines:

🩺 MBBS Curriculum

Covers human medicine, clinical pharmacology, pharmacodynamics (PD), pharmacokinetics (ADME-Tox), and biochemical reaction energetics. Features high-yield benchmarks such as Aspirin Serine acetylation of COX-1/COX-2 and Paracetamol CYP2E1 metabolism into NAPQI.

💊 Pharm-D Curriculum

Focused on pharmaceutical sciences, organic drug synthesis, transition state activation barriers (ΔG‡, ΔH‡, ΔS‡), enantioselective synthesis, esterification kinetics, and beta-lactam antibacterial mechanisms.

🐾 DVM Curriculum

Addresses veterinary medicine, comparative pharmacokinetics across species (canine, feline, equine, bovine), feline glucuronidation deficiencies, species-specific drug toxicity, and antimicrobial resistance profiles.

Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (MLIP)

Quantum Forge integrates an ensemble of cutting-edge neural network interatomic potentials and quantum chemical calculators:

  • MACE: Higher-order equivariant message passing neural network potential for atomic systems with ab initio accuracy.
  • CHGNet: Pretrained Crystal Hamiltonian Graph Neural Network for inorganic and organometallic crystal structures.
  • ANI-2x: Deep neural network potential for universal organic and medicinal chemistry.
  • GFN2-xTB: Robust semi-empirical quantum mechanical method with extended tight-binding Hamiltonian.

Users can compute activation enthalpies, transition states, reaction free energies, and vibrational partition functions in milliseconds directly from their web browser.
