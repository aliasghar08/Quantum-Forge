About Quantum Forge

Bridging quantum chemical transition-state modeling, equivariant neural network potentials, and interactive 3D biomedical simulation for researchers, educators, and medical scholars.

Launch Interactive Workstation →

1. Mission & Scientific Vision

Quantum Forge was conceived and developed by Ali Asghar to solve a fundamental educational and computational bottleneck in molecular sciences: the gap between abstract textbook chemical equations and live, physical atomistic dynamics.

In traditional medical (MBBS), pharmaceutical (Pharm-D), and chemistry curricula, students and researchers analyze two-dimensional reaction mechanisms that reduce complex continuous potential energy surfaces into static drawings. Quantum Forge transforms these static mechanisms into fully interactive, physically accurate 3D reaction trajectories driven by state-of-the-art machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs), transition-state theory (TST), and hemodynamic fluid dynamics.

2. Core Computational Architecture & Engines

Equivariant Neural Force Fields (MLIP)

Executes millisecond-scale force evaluations using E(3)-equivariant message passing architectures (MACE, PaiNN) and high-dimensional neural potentials (ANI-2x, CHGNet), achieving density functional theory (DFT) accuracy at a fraction of the computational cost.

Transition-State & Barrier Search (CI-NEB)

Implements Climbing Image Nudged Elastic Band (CI-NEB) algorithms to map minimum energy reaction paths (MEP), determine first-order saddle points, and calculate activation free energies (ΔG‡, ΔH‡, ΔS‡).

Clinical Pharmacopeia & Metabolism

Features detailed bioactivation profiles for WHO Essential Medicines, including Aspirin Ser-530 COX acetylation, Paracetamol CYP2E1 oxidation into toxic NAPQI, and N-acetylcysteine antidotal rescue kinetics.

CardioCath 3D Bedside Oscilloscope

Simulates Navier-Stokes coronary flow dynamics, Fractional Flow Reserve (FFR/iFR), and a 5-channel hospital multi-parameter bedside monitor (Dual-lead ECG, invasive ABP Pa/Pd, SpO&sub2; plethysmograph, and EtCO&sub2; capnography).

3. Peer-Reviewed Provenance & Quality Standards

Every molecular template, reaction pathway, and hemodynamic formula implemented in Quantum Forge is curated against international scientific databases and peer-reviewed literature:

  • Structural Biology: Protein Data Bank (RCSB PDB) crystallographic and cryo-EM coordinates (e.g., Ovine COX-1 PDB 1PTH, Murine COX-2 PDB 1CX2, Human hERG channel PDB 5VA1).
  • Literature Indexing: CrossRef and OpenAlex digital object identifier (DOI) resolution for all kinetic and thermodynamic benchmark datasets.
  • Pharmacogenomic Guidelines: Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium (CPIC) and Dutch Pharmacogenetics Working Group (DPWG) dosing guidelines for CYP2D6, CYP2C19, and CYP2C9 substrates.
  • Hemodynamic Validations: FFR and iFR physiologic thresholds calibrated against landmark multicenter clinical trials (DEFER, FAME I/II, DEFINE-FLAIR).
